What is eShikshaKosh Bihar

eShikshaKosh Bihar is an online portal created by the Bihar government to manage and organize educational data digitally.

It helps in maintaining teacher records, attendance tracking, salary management, and school data in one place.

Understanding the Login Process

To log in, users need to visit the official website and enter their user ID, password, and captcha code.

After successful login, users can access their dashboard where all information and features are available.

Who Uses eShikshaKosh Bihar

This platform is mainly used by teachers and administrative staff working in government schools.

Officials use it to monitor performance and manage educational systems efficiently.

How eShikshaKosh Makes Work Easier

Earlier, all records were managed manually, which was time-consuming. Now everything is digital and easily accessible.

Teachers can check salary details, attendance, and service records without visiting offices.

Role of the e ShikshaKosh App
